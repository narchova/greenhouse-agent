# My agent: greenhouse-agent
One-liner: A conversational greenhouse and plant-care assistant that helps indoor plant enthusiasts manage and care for a catalog of plants with care tips, inventory tracking, and recommendations.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers user's experience level, light conditions in their home, and favorite plant varieties.
- Tools: Firestore tools to query plant catalog, search by light/care requirements, and add new plants to inventory.
- Catalog/UI: Collection of house plants (name, species, light requirement, watering interval, care level, price) to render as cards.
- Image gen: Plant progress visuals and care condition illustrations.
- Sandbox: Watering schedule calculation and fertilizer dilution ratios.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch: code sandbox for care calculations
